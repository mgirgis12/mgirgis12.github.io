## Hardware and Software

I am studying Computer Engineering, and I have always been more interested in hardware, but I also understand how important software is. Hardware and software work together in many systems, and learning software engineering can help me become a better computer engineer. I am interested in understanding how software can be used to control hardware and solve real engineering problems.

## My Experience with AI Security

I am also interested in AI and cybersecurity. Working on projects involving LLM jailbreaking and RAG poisoning gave me experience with testing AI systems and looking for security problems. These projects showed me that software is not only about writing code. It is also important to think about security, testing, and how a system will respond when something goes wrong.

Working on these projects also helped me see how different areas of computer engineering can connect together. Even though I am more interested in hardware, having software and programming skills gives me more ways to work on different engineering problems.

## Improving My Software Skills

One of my goals is to become more comfortable with programming and software development. I want to improve my coding skills and learn how to organize larger projects. I also want more experience working with other people because engineering projects usually require teamwork and communication.

Programming can sometimes be challenging for me, especially when I am learning new syntax or trying to understand a larger program. However, I think improving these skills is important because software is used in almost every area of computer engineering.

## My Future Goals

In the future, I hope to work in an area where I can use both hardware and software skills. I am especially interested in embedded systems, hardware, AI security, cybersecurity, and other areas where software interacts with physical systems.

I want to continue learning and getting more hands-on experience. Improving my software engineering skills along with my hardware knowledge will give me more options and help prepare me for different types of engineering projects after I graduate.
